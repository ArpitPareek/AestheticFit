# AestheticFit — What the App Can Do

*A guide to everything you can do in the app, written for you (Arpit & Harshita).*

*Last updated: 27 Sep 2026*

AestheticFit is your personal fitness, nutrition, and skincare companion — an installable app on your phone (tap "Add to Home Screen" when prompted). It's built for the two of you, keeps each of your data private, and is designed to work smoothly on your phone.

Everything lives under six tabs at the bottom: **Today · Workouts · Nutrition · Track · Skin · Profile**.

---

## Getting started

- **Sign in** with your email and password. Your data is yours — nothing is shared between the two profiles.
- **First-time setup**: a friendly 8-step questionnaire learns about you — your basics (age, height, current & goal weight), your goals and priorities, your training experience, how many days and how long you can train, your equipment, exercises you love or want to avoid, any injuries, and lifestyle details (cardio you enjoy, daily steps, sleep, job type). Every step saves as you go, so you never lose progress.
- At the end, tap **"Generate My Plan"** and the app builds a full, phased training program tailored to your answers.
- You can **retake the assessment** anytime if your goals or situation change.

---

## Today — your daily home base

Open the app and the **Today** tab greets you with everything that matters right now:

- A personal greeting and which **transformation week** you're in.
- Your **activity streak** (with a kind message if it resets — no guilt trips).
- A quick-glance stats row: **protein, calories, steps, weight, and sleep** — your protein ring is front and centre.
- Tap-through cards to **today's workout** (or a rest-day view), a **nutrition snapshot** with a Quick Add button, and your **skincare** AM/PM status.
- **Smart coaching notes** that change with your journey — what to expect week by week (foundation → first neural gains → visible changes), skin-purging guidance, and a gentle nudge if your protein is low in the afternoon.

## Workouts

Your training, guided set by set.

- **Review and start your plan**: see each day laid out with exercises, sets, reps, target effort (RIR), and rest, then tap to begin.
- **Today's session** is pinned to your real training days. On rest days you get a rest screen — with a "train anyway" option if you're feeling it.
- **Log a session for any of the last 7 days** if you trained but forgot to log, or shuffled your week.
- **Exercise cards** with demo animations, coaching cues, and a "Watch on YouTube" link if you want to see the movement.
- **Warm-up and cool-down/stretch checklists** for each session, so you don't skip the important bits.
- **Log every set** — weight × reps at your chosen effort — with a **Prefill** button that fills in last session's numbers, and arrows showing whether you're improving.
- **Smart progression suggestions**: the app recommends your next weight or reps in plain language, and knows when it's time to back off (deload).
- For assisted or incline moves, it tracks the *right* kind of progress — less assistance, or a harder surface (Wall → Floor).
- **Swap an exercise** mid-session for any listed alternative (too hard, machine busy, injury, or just preference) — your plan stays intact.
- **Injury-aware warnings** flag any movement that clashes with injuries you told us about.
- **Finish your workout** to get a summary: exercises done, total volume, an **editable duration**, and an **estimated calories burned**.
- When a training phase is complete, the app invites you to **advance to the next phase** — and celebrates when you finish the whole program.

## Nutrition

Eating tracking that's fast and forgiving.

- **Just type what you ate** — "2 roti, 1 katori moong dal, small bowl dahi" — and the app's **AI breaks it into individual foods with full macros**. Each item shows whether it was matched from the food library or AI-estimated, and you can tweak quantities, edit ingredient amounts, or remove items before logging. Your draft is saved even if you close the app.
- Foods the AI figures out are **saved to "My Foods"** automatically, so next time they're instant.
- Prefer to pick from a list? Use the **searchable food library** with categories, portion sizing (½× to 2×), and a live macro preview — then add to breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snacks.
- **Manage My Foods**: edit any saved food, delete ones you don't want, and "Clean up" to merge duplicates.
- **Backdate meals** up to 7 days.
- **Deleted something by mistake?** A 5-second **Undo** bar has your back.
- **Macro dashboard** puts protein first (your daily "floor"), shows calories and per-meal breakdowns, and — if you're short on protein — suggests concrete foods to close the gap.
- **Targets** (calories, protein, carbs, fat) are auto-calculated for you, and you can customize them anytime.

## Track — everything else that moves the needle

- **Recovery Watch**: a gentle advisory that appears if signs of under-recovery show up, with the reasons why. Dismissible.
- **Progress photos**: snap front / side / back shots, browse a private timeline, and view them full-screen. Stored privately — only you can see them. You'll get a nudge roughly every 4 weeks.
- **Weight & measurements**: log weight for any date, plus waist (weekly) and hip/chest (monthly). See a **28-day smoothed average** (trust this over the daily scale wobble), your 4-week trend, and how far you are from your goal with an estimated timeline.
- **Lift progress**: see how your estimated one-rep max has climbed on your top lifts.
- **Cardio tracker**: log any activity (search the library or type your own) with minutes, distance/pace, intensity or effort (RPE), and **auto-estimated calories** you can override. Weekly readout shows your Zone-2 count and steps.
- **Daily log**: steps, sleep hours, and a tap-to-count water tracker.

## Skin & Hair

- **AM/PM routines tailored to you**, step by step, each with a checkbox and instructions — plus **built-in wait timers** for steps that need one (like letting a product absorb).
- **Tretinoin-day warnings** so you know what to expect.
- **Hair-care steps** in a tidy collapsible list.
- **Check-ins**: rate your skin's texture, evenness, hydration, and breakouts, and watch the trends over time with mini charts.

## Profile & your data

- **Wedding countdown** — set the date and see the days tick down.
- **View your active plan** day by day, and your assessment summary.
- **Edit your nutrition targets** (or reset to Auto).
- **Export all your data** as a single file, and **import it back** — your information stays portable and yours.
- **Sign out** anytime.

## The smart bits, in plain terms

- **AI meal understanding** — the standout feature: describe a meal in your own words and it becomes tracked macros.
- **Personalized training plan** built from your assessment, organized into phases.
- **Progression coaching** that tells you when to push and when to recover.
- **Calorie estimates** for both cardio and strength sessions, based on your bodyweight.
- **Recovery detection**, streaks, and week-by-week guidance that keeps you moving in the right direction.
